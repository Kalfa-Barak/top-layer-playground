Top Layer Playground

Popovers, dialogs, fullscreen, customizable select, shadow DOM, iframes, inert, and a z-index trap. The side panel tracks the top layer, focus and every event.

popover="auto" — light-dismiss, one stack at a time

Opening an unrelated auto popover closes the others. Opening one from inside another nests it. Esc unwinds one level; an outside click closes the chain.

popover="manual" — no light-dismiss, stack freely

Last opened is on top. Re-showing an open popover doesn't move it; close and reopen to bring it forward ("↑ Front").

hint, interestfor & anchoring

hint doesn't close open auto popovers: open SM first, then the hint. interestfor opens on hover/focus after a short delay.
