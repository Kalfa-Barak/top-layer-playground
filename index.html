Top Layer Playground

Popovers (auto / manual / hint), modal & non-modal dialogs, nesting, light-dismiss, and a z-index trap. The side panel shows what is currently in the top layer and logs every toggle event.

popover="auto" — light-dismiss, one stack at a time

Opening an unrelated auto popover closes the others. Opening one from inside another auto popover nests it (both stay open). Esc / outside click closes the topmost chain.

popover="manual" — no light-dismiss, stack freely

Last opened = on top. Re-showing an already-open popover does not move it; close + reopen to bring it to the front (or use the "↑ Bring to front" buttons).

popover="hint" & anchored

hint doesn't close open auto popovers (Chromium 133+; falls back to manual elsewhere). Anchoring uses CSS anchor positioning.

Dialogs

Modal dialogs make everything else inert, but popovers opened from inside the dialog still go on top of it.

z-index / stacking-context trap

This box has z-index: 999999, transform and overflow: hidden. Popovers declared inside it still escape to the top layer:
